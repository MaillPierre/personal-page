## Positions

* 2024 - 2026 : Research Engineer, P16 project, INRIA Sophia Antipolis (2 years)

Development of Corese to industrial standards, in collaboration with :Probabl..

Corese is a framework for knowledge representation and reasoning, based on Semantic Web standards, that has been developed at INRIA since 2000. In 2024, Corese was selected as a reference technology for the support of a new generation of knowledge-based systems in the context of the P16 project. In collaboration with the company Probabl., a team of five engineers is working on the development of Corese to industrial standards, with a focus on performance, scalability, and usability. The goal is to make Corese a robust and reliable open-source platform for building knowledge-based applications in various domains.

* 2021 – 2024 : Post-doc, ANR DeKaLoG project, INRIA Sophia Antipolis (3.5 years)

Modelling and development of a metadata index for online RDF databases, IndeGx. Creation of an open tool for easy dataset description, Metadatamatic.

The ANR DeKaLog project aims to support research around the vision of a decentralized and distributed Web of Data, where data is published and consumed by multiple actors in a collaborative manner. The project promotes in particular the principles of Accessibility, Transparency, and Findability. As part of this project, I have been working on the development of IndeGx, a metadata index for online RDF databases. IndeGx allows users to easily discover and access datasets published on the Web of Data by providing a centralized index of metadata about these datasets. IndeGx can be used by any expert to define their indexation rules and create an index for a specific domain. In addition, I have also developed Metadatamatic, an open tool that allows users to easily describe their datasets using standard metadata vocabularies. In collaboration with the LIRIS, we created custom rules to generate an index of publicly available knowledge graphs based on their transparency.

* 2017 – 2019 : Maturation Engineer, SEMELEV project, SATT Ouest Valorisation (1.5 years)

Maturation for industrial use of the SPARKLIS RDF base exploration prototype.

The SPARKLIS project aims to provide a user-friendly interface for exploring and querying RDF databases. As part of this project, I have been working on the maturation of the SPARKLIS prototype for industrial use. This involved improving the usability and performance of the system, as well as developing new features to support advanced querying and data visualization. The goal was to make SPARKLIS a robust and reliable tool for exploring RDF databases in various domains.

* 2015 – 2017 : Post-doc, ANR IDFraud project, IRISA/Université Rennes 1, SemLIS team, Rennes (2 years)

Design of a management system for the description of illegal ID documents. Implementation of the FORMULIS system. Experimentation with the French national gendarmerie.

The ANR IDFraud project aimed to develop approaches for the automatic detection and analysis of illegal ID documents. As part of this project, I have been working on the design and implementation of a management system for the description of illegal ID documents. This involved developing a formal model for representing the features of ID documents, as well as implementing the FORMULIS system, which allows users to easily describe ID documents by providing intelligent value suggestions based on context. The system was tested in collaboration with the French national gendarmerie. As part of this project, I have also given training to the IDnow (formerly AriadNEXT) company on knowledge graph technologies, and they have adopted them to build their own ID document management system.

The project has also lead to the development of a new approach for the evaluation of the updates and the comparison of versions of the knowledge graph by the merging of knowledge graph technologies and pattern mining techniques.

* 2012 – 2015 : Doctorate in Computer Science from the University of Angers, 26 Novembre 2015, UFR Sciences Angers (3 years)

As part of my PhD thesis, I have developed new methods for the evaluation, evolution, and querying of databases of the Web of Data. I conceived and implemented three methods to help the expansion at the factual and ontological level and the querying of bases from the Web of Data.
